# Trademarks

Lobby's code is open source under the [Mozilla Public License 2.0](LICENSE). The licence covers
the code, not the name or the look of the product: section 2.3 of the MPL says so in as many words.
This page says what that means in practice.

## What is not licensed

- The name **Lobby**, used for a media library, its server or its apps.
- The **portal icon** (the open door) and the **kids icon**, in every form and size.
- The App Store listing of Lobby: its name, screenshots and texts.

The artwork itself (icons, avatars, kids art) is all rights reserved; see
[`assets/LICENSE-ART`](assets/LICENSE-ART).

## What you may do

- Say that your work is based on Lobby, works with Lobby, or is a fork of Lobby, in plain words:
  "MyLibrary, a fork of Lobby" or "works with Lobby Server".
- Use the name to refer to Lobby itself: in articles, reviews, talks, package descriptions,
  issues and pull requests.
- Build and run the apps from this repository for yourself, with the icons as they are.
- Package Lobby Server unchanged (for example for a NAS app store or a Docker image) under the
  name Lobby Server, as long as it is clear that the package is not made by the Lobby project.

## What you may not do

- Name a fork, a derived app or a service "Lobby", or anything that can be mistaken for it.
- Use the portal icon, the kids icon, or a look-alike, for a fork or a derived app.
- Publish an app to an app store under the Lobby name or with Lobby's icons, or in a way that
  suggests it comes from the Lobby project.
- Suggest that the Lobby project endorses, supports or made your product when it did not.

A fork that is distributed needs its own name, its own icons and its own bundle identifier.
